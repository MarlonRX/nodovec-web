# CashPilot Promotional Video Design

## Brand Colors
- Background: #0f0f12 (deep dark)
- Surface: #1a1a1f
- Primary text: #e1e1e1
- Secondary text: #a0a0a0
- Accent: #d4af37 (gold)
- Accent hover: #c9a632
- Border: #2a2a30
- Success: #2e8b57
- Error: #cf666b

## Typography
- Headlines: Inter Black (900)
- Body: Inter Regular (400)
- Fallback: system-ui, sans-serif

## Motion Style
- High energy for promos
- Snappy transitions (0.2-0.3s)
- Gold accent glows
- Professional finance app feel

## Video Structure (10s)
1. Scene 1 (0-2.5s): Logo slam + tagline
2. Scene 2 (2.5-5s): Dashboard feature
3. Scene 3 (5-7.5s): Transactions feature
4. Scene 4 (7.5-10s): CTA final
